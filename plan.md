# Space Adventure - Future Ideas Plan

## 1. Cybernetic Implants / Augmentation System
**How it works:** Introduce a new progression layer separate from leveling and traditional equipment. Players have a limited number of "Cyber-Slots" (e.g., Head, Arms, Torso, Nervous System). 
**Why it fits:** It leans heavily into the retro sci-fi theme. 
**Mechanics:**
- **Reflex Boosters:** Grants a chance to start combat with +1 Action Point (AP).
- **Sub-dermal Plating:** Converts 10% of incoming physical damage into energy drain instead of health loss.
- **Targeting Matrix:** Increases the "Assassinate" or "Power Strike" critical multiplier.
- Implants could be permanent unless removed by a specialized "Cyber-Doc" NPC, adding a strategic money-sink.

## 2. Deep Space Dungeons: "Derelict Ships & Anomalies"
**How it works:** When using the travel system between planets, there is a chance to intercept a distress signal from a procedurally generated derelict spaceship or spatial anomaly. 
**Why it fits:** It builds upon the existing "Random Events" and "Location-Specific Loot" systems. 
**Mechanics:**
- **Survival Mechanic:** These areas have no life support. The player has a limited amount of Oxygen/Energy (functioning as a turn limit) to explore rooms, fight unique alien infestations, and find high-tier loot.
- **Risk vs. Reward:** The deeper the player goes into the derelict ship, the better the loot (and chance for Epic/Legendary items you plan to add), but if they run out of oxygen, they fail the run and lose what they found.

## 3. Faction Reputation & Allegiances
**How it works:** Expand the existing quest and NPC systems by introducing major galactic factions (e.g., The Galactic Federation, The Void Corsairs [Space Pirates], and the Photon Prime Syndicate). 
**Why it fits:** Adds narrative depth and gives more weight to the "Main Story" and "Side Quests" systems. 
**Mechanics:**
- Completing quests for one faction raises your reputation with them but lowers it with their rivals.
- **Perks:** High reputation with Photon Prime might give a 20% discount on online orders and exclusive high-tier drop boxes.
- **Consequences:** Negative reputation with the Void Corsairs means you get ambushed by pirate boarding parties during planetary travel.
