* **Role:** Cyborg Scrapper
* **Faction:** Galactic Federation
* **Signature Skill:** Shield Generator (+5 DEF, 3-turn duration)

* **Role:** Android Medic
* **Faction:** Photon Prime Syndicate
* **Signature Skill:** Nano-Heal (+25 HP, 4-turn cooldown)

### How to use these in the game:
In a future update, these portraits can be copied to the `assets/` directory and loaded by the UI components:
* **Companions UI:** Display the companion avatars next to their quarters status cards in [`companions-ui.js`](file:///d:/source/Roogames/Space%20Adventure/systems/ui/companions-ui.js).
* **Dialogue Panel:** Display Vance, Nesta, or Thorne's portraits during branching narrative conversations in [`dialogue-ui.js`](file:///d:/source/Roogames/Space%20Adventure/systems/ui/dialogue-ui.js).
* **Combat Overlay:** Render the Void Sentinel Alpha's central core illustration when entering the boss phase in [`combat.js`](file:///d:/source/Roogames/Space%20Adventure/systems/combat.js).
